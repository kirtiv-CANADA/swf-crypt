# SWF Crypt - Legal and content rules

- Only use SWF files that are public domain, created by the project owner, or
  explicitly licensed for redistribution.
- Every sample must be listed in `samples/LICENSES.md` with source, author and license.
- Do NOT build features that bypass, crack, strip or unpack DRM or protection
  schemes (for example anything that "unpacks" protected SWF files).
- Uploaded files are played locally in the browser and are not republished.
- Credit Ruffle (MIT / Apache-2.0) in the README and on the About page.
- No copyrighted music, images or trademarks in the app or the demo video.
- Project name: "SWF Crypt". Do not use the Adobe or Ruffle names in the title.
